`pie-item-player` takes `passage-layout="splitpane"` to render a stimulus passage beside the item instead of above it, stacking again below a player width of 768px. With `allowed-resize` the split gets a draggable, keyboard-operable divider. The default, `vertical`, keeps the stacked layout.
