Items whose only numbers are dates, year ranges or similar (`10/10/2026`, `1990-2000`, `1809 - 1865`, `555-1234`), or statistics such as "40% of voters", no longer count as mathematical content, so they no longer get the calculator. A percentage still counts when taken of a number, beside an operator, or in text with a money amount.
