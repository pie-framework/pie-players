`--pie-tertiary` link text now holds 4.5:1 on the raised surface (`--pie-surface`)
as well as on the page, as a named contrast relationship ("raised surface link
text") that every built-in scheme and registered palette is checked against.
Black on Rose's link colour moves from `#990044` to `#82003a`, and Black on
Violet's from `#4a1259` to its own primary-dark `#330d3f`; both were under 3.9:1
on their raised surface.
