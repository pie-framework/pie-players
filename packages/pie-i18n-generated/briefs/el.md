# Greek (el) terminology brief

Serves every Greek host: the catalog is registered under the bare `el` tag,
so `el`, `el-GR` and `el-CY` widen to it. Register is the Greek of schools
and Greek assessment software — the Microsoft-Greek register Greek Windows,
Office and Greek e-learning platforms print — not a generic tech
localization.

## Register

- **Controls carry the noun form where Greek software has one**:
  `Κλείσιμο`, `Υποβολή`, `Αποθήκευση`, `Διαγραφή`, `Μετακίνηση`,
  `Επανάληψη`, `Επισήμανση` — the Greek Windows/Office convention.
  Greek has no infinitive, so there is no infinitive to reach for.
- **Where no natural noun exists, controls and instructions take the polite
  imperative plural** (`Δοκιμάστε`, `Επιλέξτε`, `Πληκτρολογήστε`,
  `Σύρετε`) — the Microsoft-Greek dialog register. Never the singular
  imperative (`Κλείσε`, `Δοκίμασε`).
- **Error prose follows the Microsoft-Greek pattern**
  `Δεν ήταν δυνατή η φόρτωση…`, `Δεν ήταν δυνατή η εκκίνηση…` —
  impersonal, agreeing with the feminine action noun.
- **State changes are authored as aorist event sentences**, never state
  fragments or participles: `Άνοιξε η βασική αριθμομηχανή`,
  `Η απάντηση καταγράφηκε`, `Μετακινήθηκε προς τα πάνω στη θέση {position}`,
  `Περιστράφηκε στις {degrees} μοίρες`.
- Whole sentences authored whole; no fragment assembly.

## Grammar and orthography pins

- **Genderless enums take the neuter**: `all` = Όλα, `none` = Κανένα,
  `any` = Οποιοδήποτε. Where a form agrees with a fixed grammatical subject,
  it follows that subject: `(αποκλεισμένη)` agrees with `επιλογή`,
  `τοπική`/`απομακρυσμένη`/`Ανδρική`/`Γυναικεία` agree with `φωνή`,
  `Άγνωστη` agrees with `κατηγορία`.
- **Τελικό ν (final nu) applied per the school rule**: kept before vowels
  and plosives (`στην ερώτηση`, `αυτήν την ενότητα`, `την emphasis`), dropped
  elsewhere (`στη μονάδα`).
- **ς/σ**: word-final sigma is always ς (`Αναζήτηση`, `διαθέσιμη`); σ only
  word-initially and word-internally.
- **Sentence-internal field labels stay lowercase** (`σύμβολο:`,
  `ατομικός αριθμός:`, `κατηγορία:` in `elementA11y`); standalone panel
  labels keep their capital (`Τύπος:`).
- **Maths register**: a formula is a `μαθηματικός τύπος`; function graphs
  and charts are `γραφήματα` — the word Excel Greek prints for a chart.
- Mid-string nouns stay lowercase except proper names (`Περιοδικός πίνακας`
  capitalizes as the tool name; `περιοδικός πίνακας` in prose).

## Selection announcements (annotation live region)

The annotation strip announces what a learner just did, for screen readers.
The strings carry a `{text}` placeholder holding the learner's selection and
follow the register rules above, with these pinned shapes:

- The availability announcement names the toolbar by its own name and stays
  impersonal, with the follow-up command in the polite imperative plural:
  `Η γραμμή εργαλείων σχολιασμού κειμένου είναι διαθέσιμη για «{text}».
  Πατήστε Shift+F10 για τα εργαλεία σχολιασμού.` (`Shift+F10` stays Latin).
- Colour, underline and removal announcements are aorist events with the
  neuter article before the quoted selection, the colour as `με` plus the
  bare colour word, and no trailing periods (the English they mirror have
  none): `Το «{text}» επισημάνθηκε με κίτρινο`,
  `Το «{text}» υπογραμμίστηκε`, `Το σχόλιο αφαιρέθηκε από το «{text}»`
  (`σχόλιο`, matching `removeA11y`).
- `clearedA11y` inflects verb and noun with the count:
  `Απαλείφθηκε {count} σχόλιο / Απαλείφθηκαν {count} σχόλια`
  (`απαλοιφή`, matching `clearAll`).

## Punctuation

- Quotes are the Greek guillemets `«»`, applied in `lookingUp`, `noEntryFor`
  and the custom-provider notices; never Latin curly quotes.
- The ano teleia `·` is the Greek sentence semicolon, applied where English
  uses `;` between clauses (`liveUpdatesDisconnected`, `speedOptionsHelp`).
- The `;` glyph appears **only** as a quoted literal in `speedOptionsHelp`
  (`με «,» ή με «;»`), naming the accepted input separators by glyph: the
  Greek keyboard's `;` key is the Greek question mark (ερωτηματικό), and the
  app parses the Latin `;`, so naming it `άνω τελεία` would mislead.
  The Greek question mark does not otherwise arise in this catalog.
- The `…` ellipsis and the `°` glyph are kept.

## Untranslated product names

PIE, PNP, TTS, SSML, WaveNet, Studio, Desmos, Polly, AWS, Google Cloud TTS,
Web Speech API, HTTP, JSON, API, MP3, OGG, PCM, Hz, SSML tags named in prose
(`emphasis`), and `backend`/`host` in developer panels.

## Plurals

- CLDR categories `one`/`other`, both carried explicitly on all six plural
  groups, and **Greek genuinely inflects**: `λήμμα/λήμματα`,
  `εικόνα/εικόνες`, `φωνή/φωνές`, `προσπάθεια/προσπάθειες`, and the verbs
  agree with the count (`Απομένει/Απομένουν`, `εντοπίστηκε/εντοπίστηκαν`).
  `{count}` is present in every category.
- Arabic numerals everywhere (`{count}`, `{position}`, `{degrees}` render as
  digits).
- Greek is LTR; nothing here overrides direction.

## A11y strings

Keys ending `A11y` are screen-reader operating instructions. Author them as
complete, self-sufficient sentences — the learner hears them without seeing
the layout — and keep every keyboard instruction concrete. Latin key names
stay in Latin, as Greek keyboards label them: Shift, PageUp, PageDown,
Escape, +/-, U. Arrow keys are `βέλη` / `πλήκτρα βέλους`, the name Greek
Windows prints.
