# Spanish (es) brief

Serves every Spanish host: the catalog is registered under the bare `es` tag,
so `es`, `es-MX`, `es-419` and `es-ES` all widen to it. Content flavour is
**neutral Latin-American Spanish**, the register of US Spanish-language
assessment and LatAm edtech.

## Register

- Controls carry the impersonal infinitive or the unstated-`usted`
  imperative typical of Spanish UI: `Cerrar`, `Buscar`, `Intentar de nuevo`.
- Sentences prefer impersonal phrasing (`No se pudo…`, `No se pueden
  restaurar…`); possessives use `tus` only where a possessive is unavoidable,
  matching informal Spanish edtech (`tus respuestas guardadas`).
- Whole sentences authored whole; no fragment assembly.

## Plurals, punctuation

- CLDR `one`/`other`, like English.
- Keep the `…` ellipsis, curly quotes and the `°` glyph; Spanish question
  punctuation does not arise in this catalog.
