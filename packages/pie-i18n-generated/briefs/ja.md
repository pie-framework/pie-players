# Japanese (ja) brief

Serves every Japanese host under the bare `ja` tag. Register follows
Japanese edtech UI conventions.

## Register

- Controls carry the verb-stem/noun labels standard in Japanese UI:
  `閉じる` (close), `キャンセル` (cancel), `保存` (save), `提出` (submit),
  `完了` (finish).
- Sentences use です・ます form (`~しました`, `~してください`).
- Whole sentences authored whole; no fragment assembly.

## Plurals, punctuation, digits

- Japanese has a single CLDR plural category: every plural group carries
  `other` only.
- Full-width punctuation in Japanese contexts (，＋（）); keep `…`, the `°`
  glyph and Latin product names as-is.
